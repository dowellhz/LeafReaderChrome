# Privacy policy

LeafReader Chrome stores captured webpages, annotations, vocabulary, reading
preferences, and AI conversation history locally in the browser.

- Reading data is not sent to LeafReader Chrome servers.
- Selected text and relevant reading context are sent only when the reader
  explicitly invokes an AI action, and only to the AI provider configured in
  Settings.
- API keys remain in extension-local storage and are excluded from backups by
  default.
- The bundled offline dictionary is queried locally and makes no network
  request.

The extension requests access to HTTP and HTTPS pages solely to provide its
selection toolbar, original-page markers, and reader-mode capture on pages the
reader chooses to use.
